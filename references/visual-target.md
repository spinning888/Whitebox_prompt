# Declared visual targets and proxy leakage

The former broad finished-neutral-v2 phrase did not define an appearance class.
Treat it as a legacy unspecified-style policy, not a sufficient leakage-evaluation
standard. Do not silently change its meaning in historical runs.

For a realistic-human benchmark, explicitly select and freeze
`finished-live-action-v1` across the bank:

“Realistic live-action appearance, anatomically coherent human subjects, finished
scene surfaces, physically plausible materials and coherent illumination and contact
shadows across subjects, props and environment.”

This is an authored target, not an observation from the whitebox. Appearance references
control assigned identity/wardrobe attributes. Unspecified design may vary within the
declared class without changing structural layout or events. A cartoon, mannequin
or visibly unfinished proxy human does not meet this live-action target. A plastic
object, white wall or minimal set is not leakage merely because it resembles a proxy
colour: assess its declared role, finish and reference scope. Record appearance-style
noncompliance separately from specifically identifiable proxy features retained.

The generic skill does not force live action on animation, object-only or other
tasks. For those tasks declare a separate common style policy and its observable
acceptance criteria. Without a chosen target, drafting is possible but appearance
class and proxy-leakage scoring remain pending specification. Do not issue a numeric
leakage metric without a separate operational scoring protocol and calibration.
