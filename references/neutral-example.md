flat rectangular object to the other; the receiver holds it at completion; mostly
stationary camera; one continuous view. No first-frame ownership, spatial arrangement,
phase times, contact mechanics or end-hold duration is supplied. Appearance and audio
are unknown. This example tests restraint only, not detailed film authoring ability.

- Observed: the supplied facts above.
- Supported interpretation: A is the transferring role, B the receiving role.
- Target: explicitly selected finished-live-action-v1, not inferred from the proxy.
- Unknown: initial positions/ownership, response onset, contact sequence, phase timing,
